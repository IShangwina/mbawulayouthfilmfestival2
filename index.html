Queer Focus at Mbawula 2025

This year, the Mbawula Youth Film Festival proudly shines a spotlight on powerful queer narratives, continuing its mission to amplify youth voices through bold and authentic storytelling. In a country and continent where queer visibility is often contested, Mbawula creates a vital platform for conversation, empathy, and celebration through the cinematic lens.

The 2025 edition presents a carefully curated lineup of films that unapologetically center LGBTQIA+ stories, identities, and lived experiences—ranging from deeply personal journeys to reflections on systemic struggles and intimate moments of love and resistance.

Onthou Vi Fredo

On June 29, 2023, Alfredo April, a beloved drag artist, queer icon, and respected member of the Malmesbury community, was tragically murdered near his home. Despite this profound loss, his case remains unsolved, stalled by missing evidence and delayed DNA results, leaving justice indefinitely postponed.

Onthou Vi Fredo? delves beyond the case, painting an intimate portrait of Alfredo through the memories of his family, friends, and the Malmesbury community. Affectionately known on stage as ‘Anushka De Patre’, Alfredo’s vibrant spirit endures through his legacy, a beacon of unapologetic self-expression, acceptance, and love.

Blending archival footage with heartfelt reflections and tributary drag performances, the documentary preserves Alfredo’s memory while honouring his role as a pillar of the queer community. It is a tribute to Alfredo’s light and an invitation to carry it forward.
