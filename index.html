STORIES THAT BRING US TOGETHER.

Mbawula Youth Film Festival is a creative platform that celebrates African storytelling, filmmaking and the power of cinema to connect communities.

Inspired by the tradition of gathering around a fire to share stories, Mbawula creates a space where filmmakers, audiences and young creatives can exchange ideas, explore perspectives and imagine new possibilities through film.

AFROFUTURISM: REIMAGINING OUR STORIES FOR THE FUTURE.

What if we could imagine Africa beyond the limitations of the present?

What would our communities look like if we designed them around our own knowledge, cultures, languages and aspirations?

This year's theme invites filmmakers and audiences to rethink how Africa is represented and to explore the possibilities of African futures through film.

Our histories are sources of knowledge. Our cultures are resources for innovation. Our young people are not merely recipients of the future; they are its creators.

What stories do we need to tell today to help us imagine the future we want tomorrow?
