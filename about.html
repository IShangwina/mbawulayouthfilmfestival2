GET TO KNOW US

Stories have the power to connect us.

Discover the people, purpose and creative vision behind the Mbawula Youth Film Festival.

01 / OUR STORY

A platform for stories, creativity and possibility.

Mbawula Youth Film Festival is a platform that celebrates the art of filmmaking and the power of storytelling.

Created by Awakening Creative Projects, the festival brings together filmmakers, audiences and creative communities through film, education and creative exchange.

At its heart, Mbawula seeks to create opportunities for emerging filmmakers to share their work, develop their voices and engage with the wider film industry.

Celebrating stories. Building connections.

02 / OUR PURPOSE
01

Our Vision

To contribute to a vibrant and inclusive creative landscape where stories from diverse communities are seen, heard and celebrated.

02

Our Mission

To use film as a bridge between education and entertainment while creating opportunities for emerging filmmakers, encouraging creative expression and nurturing a culture of storytelling.

03 / WHAT WE BELIEVE

Film is more than what happens on screen.

Representation

Making room for different perspectives, experiences and voices.

Education

Encouraging learning, curiosity and the development of creative skills.

Community

Creating spaces where people can connect, exchange ideas and experience stories together.

Opportunity

Supporting emerging creatives and opening pathways into the world of filmmaking.

04 / THE ORGANISATION

Awakening Creative Projects

Awakening Creative Projects is the creative organisation behind the Mbawula Youth Film Festival. Through initiatives that encourage artistic expression, participation and creative development, the organisation works to create opportunities within the creative sector.
