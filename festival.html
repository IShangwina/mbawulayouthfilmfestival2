THE AFROFUTURISM EXPERIENCE

A gathering of stories, people and possibilities.

Mbawula Youth Film Festival is a celebration of film as a medium for storytelling, education, expression and connection.

The festival brings together filmmakers, young creatives, audiences and communities to experience cinema, exchange ideas and explore the creative possibilities of our time.

Inspired by the tradition of gathering around a fire to share stories, Mbawula creates a space where different voices and perspectives can come together.

2026 FESTIVAL THEME

AFROFUTURISM: REIMAGINING OUR STORIES FOR THE FUTURE.

What if we could imagine Africa beyond the limitations of the present?

This year's theme invites filmmakers and audiences to explore African identity, imagination, technology, culture and the possibilities of tomorrow.

Our histories are sources of knowledge. Our cultures are resources for innovation. Our young people are creators of the future.
